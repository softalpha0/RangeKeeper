How the vault works

Each vault is the sole Uniswap v3 liquidity provider for its pair. It opens one concentrated-liquidity band and recenters it — closes it, reopens it nearer the live price — as conditions change. VaultManager is the keeper entry point: every recenter re-derives its own trigger, sanity check, and cooldown from RiskMath rather than trusting a keeper's proof at face value, and a real circuit breaker closes the band and sits in cash rather than chasing a genuine volatility spike.

73/73 Foundry tests pass, including a full real-pool recenter cycle and a real circuit-breaker pause + resume. See the overview for the full mechanism.
