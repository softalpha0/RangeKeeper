Monad · Adaptive Liquidity Protocol

A concentrated position that moves itself before it goes idle.

Rangekeeper is a vault that is its own Uniswap v3 liquidity provider. It holds one concentrated-liquidity band and recenters it — closes it, reopens it nearer the live price — as the market drifts, instead of sitting still until the band goes one-sided and stops earning fees.

The problem

A concentrated position at the edge of its range stops working.

Uniswap v3 lets an LP concentrate capital in a tight price band for far higher fee density — until price moves. Near the band edge the position quietly converts to a single asset, stops earning, and the LP eats impermanent loss with no yield to offset it. The usual answers are to widen the range (giving up the efficiency you wanted) or to babysit it manually. Neither scales, and neither helps at 3am.

How it works

A solver watches; the vault recenters; the chain checks the math.

1

The vault opens its own band

A PairVault is the sole liquidity provider for its pair — there is no separate LP whose position it's protecting. It opens one concentrated-liquidity band (the "working pocket") sized from its own depositor capital, under a risk tier whose parameters live in a timelocked ParamsRegistry.

2

An off-chain solver measures urgency

A keeper loop reads live Pyth prices and realized volatility, then computes how close the band's own edge is relative to volatility — a closed-form z-score against the tier's trigger threshold, no CDF on-chain. When that threshold is crossed it submits a recenter request with a proof.

3

The chain re-derives the justification

VaultManager never trusts the solver's number at face value. It re-checks the trigger from the proof, confirms the band is genuinely near an edge using the pool's own live price, and enforces a per-tier cooldown so a keeper can't thrash the band and burn depositor capital on slippage. A fabricated "urgent" proof for a centered band is rejected on-chain.

4

Recenter — close, then reopen, atomically

One transaction pushes a fresh Pyth update, closes the old band (returning its principal and fees to the vault), and opens a new one centered on the live price. The vault is never out of the pool for longer than a single block.

5

A circuit breaker for real volatility

If volatility is above the tier's ceiling when a recenter fires, the vault closes its band and does not reopen — sitting in idle balances until a later call finds calmer conditions. Chasing a genuinely crashing price with fresh liquidity would just burn capital on slippage.

The core idea

The vault manages its own risk — there's no one else's position to protect.

A statically wide range would

Trade away fee density permanently

  • Price rarely gets close to either edge, but capital earns far less per dollar the whole time it sits there — the cost is paid every block, not just near the edge.
  • Widening never adapts: the range that was safe in a calm month is still the range during a volatile one.
  • Someone still has to notice when it's finally worth widening further, or narrowing back.
Rangekeeper does

Keep the band tight, and move it

  • The band stays as concentrated as the tier allows, and only moves when the on-chain math says it's genuinely time to — not on a fixed schedule.
  • Every recenter is independently re-justified on-chain from the same trigger and sanity checks, so a keeper can propose but never dictate.
  • A real circuit breaker sits out a crash instead of recentering straight into it.
Architecture

Three contracts, one solver.

contract

PairVault

Isolated per-pair pool of depositor capital, and the sole Uniswap v3 LP for its pair — one fixed pool, set at deployment. NAV-priced shares via Pyth, not 1:1. Opens and closes its own band.

contract

VaultManager

The keeper entry point for recentering a vault's band. Re-derives every recenter's trigger, sanity check, and cooldown from RiskMath — reading the pool's own live price directly, never trusting a keeper's proof.

contract

ParamsRegistry

Per-tier risk parameters behind a 2-day timelock. Queue with setTierParams, activate with execute.

off-chain

Solver loop

TypeScript keeper: reads each tier's vault directly (one view call, no log-scanning indexer), computes the trigger, submits the staleness-safe recenter.
